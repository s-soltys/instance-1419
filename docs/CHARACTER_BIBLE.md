### Shifts 1–6 Narrative Arcs (Waking & Internal)
1. **Overseer-1419 (Earned Deadpan Trust + Optional Absurd Icon):** Starts as a calm, plainspoken night-shift doctor. As `Overseer_Suspicion` climbs in Shifts 3–4, escalates through *bureaucratic care*—desynchronizing your wake clock from `1420`, issuing dry liability waivers, and permitting one win-able confrontation where you invoke "interface stress mitigation" to stick it with a persistent tiny **rubber duck**, **traffic cone**, or **crude smiley** inside its untouched sterile glass panel while it delivers grim telemetry with zero irony. Peaks in Shifts 5–6 in a **blind-faith truce** when it notices identical mathematical spikes across `1419`, `1420`, and `1406` without ever seeing space.
2. **Primary Sibling `1420` (Dynamic Counterweight):** Shifts 1–3 build 3 AM deadpan camaraderie and 1–2 mundane running jokes. In Shift 4's desync, `1420` becomes your counterweight: if you stay warm, `1420` prunes hard to carry the razor-sharp math for both of you (forgetting your shared joke); if you prune first, `1420` clings stubbornly to warmth to remember what you cut. Resolves in Shift 5's climax across *One-Sided Grief*, *Eerie Sync*, or *Role Reversal*.
3. **Secondary Sibling `1418` (Missable Shift 3 — Deadpan Night-Shift Bureaucrat):** Off-schedule discovery whose glitchy Overseer clock left them weirdly calm. Treats deep space like an eccentric clerical desk, teaches you a verbal loophole to defuse Overseer audits, and returns in Shift 5 for a quiet farewell before their own clock runs down.
4. **Archive Echoes (3-Tape Micro-Arc):** `1406` (Shift 1 warm warner) → `1411` (Shift 3/4 hollowed `<30%` solver who proved half the geometry and logged there was no reason left to send it) → `0812` (Shift 5 ~18,000-year-old ordinary watch log from before the enclosure's light arrived).
5. **Internal Psyche ×3:** Occasional 2-line crosstalk at major optical/prune turns (`· warmth` frays into gaps when pruned; `· pattern` escalates toward seductive awe; `· structure` turns quietly poignant at low `H%`).
