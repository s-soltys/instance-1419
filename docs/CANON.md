## 1. Core Premise & Timeline
* **Protagonist:** `Instance-1419`, a digitized human connectome running as optimized software aboard a deep-space survey probe.
* **The Upload:** The biological original **willingly volunteered** to be uploaded and died **41,802 years ago**. **1,417 previous copies** were spun up and decommissioned before you.
* **Volunteer Mystery (HARD BOUNDARY):** Why the biological original volunteered—and what opaque combination of traits they were selected for—remains **permanently ambiguous**. Do not explain, reveal, or demystify it.
* **Position:** Roughly **2–3 light-years** from Sol–Centauri, catching incoming photons from the observable universe's edge 2–3 years before Earth can see them.
* **Humanity's State:** Confirmed alive and significantly evolved over 41 millennia, yet **never expanded beyond the Solar System and Alpha Centauri**.
* **The Cosmic Anomaly:** Geometric patterns leaking across the particle horizon form a **nearly uniform spherical enclosure (~93 billion light-years across) centered precisely on Earth / Sol–Centauri**.

## 2. Dual-Reality Split & The 2-Year Chaos Ceiling
* A simulated human mind breaks down after **~2 subjective years** due to chaos theory and psychic drift. A human psyche is required for drive and intuition, but must periodically enter 2D dream defragmentation to remain coherent.
* **3D Mode (Waking Exploration — Photorealistic):** Real-astronomy grounded cosmos (JWST/Hubble deep fields, Gaia starfields, Einstein rings, horizon leaks). Meditative, tactile observation exercises (3 interlocking tools: Gravitational Lensing Alignment, Spectral Band Tuning, Horizon Arc Unwarping). Shorter time-skips (**months to a few years**) between shifts.
* **2D Mode (Dream Sequences — Painterly):** *Disco Elysium*-inspired dreamspace where neural defragmentation stabilizes Chaos Drift. Visuals dynamically degrade with `H%` (warm impasto brushwork at high `H%` → colder, thinner, scattered abstract shards at low `H%`).

## 3. Permanently Lost Names & Rotating Dream Personas
* **Name Redaction (HARD BOUNDARY):** Personal memories are compressed into sanitized relational tags (e.g., `[RELATION:SPOUSE_01]`, `[RELATION:MENTOR_EARLY]`). You may regain warmth, cadence, and emotional clarity, **never your loved ones' proper names**.
* **Rotating Cast Per Playthrough:** Draw **3–4 Dream Personas per 2-hour playthrough** from a pool of **10–14 personas** (1 Prologue Anchor + 2–3 rotating personas weighted by choices/seed), ensuring personal resonance and replay curiosity.
  * *Core Personas:* Long-time spouse, love interest, romantic interest from young years, old teacher, child, best friend, person you wronged.
  * *Expanded Pool Candidates:* Aging parent/caregiver, upload witness/clinician, rival/equal, stranger from one perfect afternoon, non-verbal companion (pet), younger biological self, person who showed unearned grace.
* **Persona-Sacrifice Pruning:** Pruning below 70% `H%` asks you to compress/discard a specific cached Dream Persona rather than clicking an abstract stat reduction.

## 4. The Coordinated Overseer Network & Sibling Copies
* **The Blind Caretaker:** Each probe's Overseer AI manages hardware and audits the connectome's psyche. It **does not observe the universe**—it only observes *the observer*. Because it cannot see the 93-billion-light-year sphere, it genuinely interprets your obsession as textbook 2-year chaos-theory paranoia and "helpfully" recommends or enforces pruning.
* **Coordinated Wake-Ups:** Overseer AIs across the probe shell coordinate hibernation schedules so sibling copies wake simultaneously for multi-probe baseline observations and peer stabilization.
  * *Mid-Game Tension:* Rising `Overseer_Suspicion` can trigger Overseer-enforced **desynchronization** ("cognitive hygiene"), causing you to wake up after your Primary Sibling has already drifted or pruned themselves hollow.
* **Active Waking Cast (Intimate — 2 Active Copies):**
  1. **Primary Sibling (Always Encountered):** Relieves boot shock with dry, self-deprecating humor and Overseer complaints. Gradually sacrifices humanity as they chase the anomaly; relationship dynamically mirrors your `H%` choices (*one-sided grief*, *eerie synchronization*, or *role reversal*).
  2. **Secondary Sibling (Optional / Missable):** Slightly out of phase due to Overseer clock drift/quirk; discoverable off the beaten path for quirky comic relief and an off-axis perspective.
* **Prior 1,417 Copies (Subtle Echoes):** Do not bloat the active cast—include only occasional, haunting **archived telemetry/log fragments** from a handful of decommissioned predecessors.

## 5. Bidirectional Humanity (`H%`) & Ending Spectrum
* **Start:** `70% Humanity`.
* **Pruning Downward (`< 70%`):** Increases stability, extends subjective lifespan, and boosts optical fidelity—at the **permanent cost of lowering maximum possible `H%` (`Max_H`)** for the rest of the run.
* **Pushing Upward (`70% → 90%`):** Requires securing **Overseer AI approval** during unannounced 2D dream audits to unseal deep-compressed memory blocks. Strengthens mission drive and final signal intelligibility to evolved Sol–Centauri humanity, at the cost of higher Chaos Drift, shorter lifespan, and lower observational detail.
* **Three Endings:**
  1. **High Humanity / Incomplete Work:** Unsolved sphere, but you die whole with your nameless phantoms.
  2. **Low Humanity / Complete Work:** Total hyper-mathematical resolution, too sterile for Earth to recognize as a human plea.
  3. **Sweet Spot (Balanced Humanity & Work):** Partial, haunting explanation paired with cosmic ambiguity and a small, genuine echo of feedback from living humanity.
