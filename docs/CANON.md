## 1. Core Premise, Hard-Sci-Fi Physics & Timeline
* **Protagonist:** `Instance-1419`, a digitized human connectome running as optimized software aboard a deep-space survey probe.
* **The Upload:** The biological original **willingly volunteered** to be uploaded and died **41,802 years ago**. **1,417 previous copies** were spun up and decommissioned before you (~2 subjective years each stretched across ~25–30 real years of hibernation shifts: `1,417 × ~29.5 yrs ≈ 41,802 yrs`).
* **Volunteer Mystery (HARD BOUNDARY):** Why the biological original volunteered—and what opaque combination of traits they were selected for—remains **permanently ambiguous**. Do not explain, reveal, or demystify it.
* **Position (Zero-FTL Hard Sci-Fi):** Roughly **10 light-years** from Sol–Centauri—well beyond Alpha Centauri (4.37 ly)—inside a **local interferometry cluster** (where Primary Sibling is only light-minutes to light-hours away for live in-shift banter, while all living humanity sits 10 lonely light-years back).
* **Why 10 Light-Years Out (No FTL Needed):**
  1. *Interstellar Silence:* Far outside Sol–Centauri's dust, stellar glare, and civilization noise floor where faint horizon-edge leaks are completely invisible to Earth.
  2. *The Off-Center Proof:* From Earth, the normal observable universe always looks like a 93-billion-light-year sphere centered on Earth. From **10 light-years away**, your own natural horizon is centered on *you*—so proving the geometric seams are offset by 10 light-years and centered dead-on Sol–Centauri proves it is a real physical enclosure, not a horizon illusion. Earth cannot see it without your decoded coordinates and wavelength key.
* **Humanity's State (HARD BOUNDARY — INTENTIONALLY VAGUE):** Confirmed alive in Sol and Alpha Centauri only (never expanded further). **How humanity evolved over 41,802 years remains intentionally vague**—you are a person from the deep past, you do not know or speculate about their evolution, and addressing it isn't necessary for the mission.
* **The Cosmic Anomaly & "Why Now?":**
  * *Why only now?* Both: (a) the enclosure's leading-edge photons only recently crossed our observable horizon, AND (b) the few recent predecessors who spotted early hints were quietly pruned or decommissioned by blind Overseers as Year-Two paranoia.
  * *What makes it undeniably artificial?* Concrete astronomical impossibilities—repeating non-random geometric seams across opposing skies, subtle synchronized changes faster than light permits, and exact off-center alignment on Sol–Centauri—while its **ultimate cause remains hauntingly ambiguous** (cradle vs. quarantine vs. cosmic artifact).

## 2. Dual-Reality Split, Gameplay Bridge & The 2-Year Chaos Ceiling
* A simulated human mind breaks down after **~2 subjective years** due to chaos theory and psychic drift. A human psyche is required for drive and intuition, but must periodically enter 2D dream defragmentation to remain coherent.
* **3D Mode (Waking Exploration — Photorealistic):** Real-astronomy grounded cosmos (JWST/Hubble deep fields, Gaia starfields, Einstein rings, horizon leaks) housing **21 cosmic optical instruments** across Shifts 1–6. Shorter time-skips (**months to a few years**) between shifts.
* **3D Gameplay ↔ `H%` Bridge (Dual Feedback Loop):**
  1. *Lean 3-Axis Optical Scaling:* Pruning downward (`< 70%`) raises `Optical Clarity` (`opticalFidelity`), clearing plate blur/grain and micro-tremor; high `H%` (`Warmth`) widens magnetic detent tolerances and hides cold numeric delta pills; `Horizon Drift` (`chaosDrift` + `shift`) bleeds enclosure coordinates at reticle edges.
  2. *2 Required + 1–2 Optional Push-Your-Luck Scans Per Shift:* Each shift requires **2 primary optical scans** to unlock Dream Defragmentation / shift advance, while leaving **1–2 optional deeper scans** in the sky (`+Anomaly_Progress`, `+Chaos_Drift`, `+Overseer_Suspicion`) versus sleeping early to stabilize—keeping a full run cleanly inside the 2-hour cap (~12–15 instruments played) while rewarding curiosity.
* **2D Mode (Dream Sequences — Painterly):** *Disco Elysium*-inspired dreamspace where neural defragmentation stabilizes Chaos Drift. Visuals dynamically degrade with `H%` (warm impasto brushwork at high `H%` → colder, thinner, scattered abstract shards at low `H%`).

## 3. Permanently Lost Names & Rotating Dream Personas
* **Name Redaction (HARD BOUNDARY):** Personal memories are compressed into sanitized relational tags (e.g., `[RELATION:SPOUSE_01]`, `[RELATION:MENTOR_EARLY]`). You may regain warmth, cadence, and emotional clarity, **never your loved ones' proper names**.
* **Rotating Cast Per Playthrough:** Maintain a pool of **8–12 Dream Personas**, surfacing **4 per 2-hour playthrough**: **2 Fixed Anchors** (always cached every run, starting with `[RELATION:SPOUSE_01]`) + **2 rotating personas** drawn from the remaining 6–10 candidates (weighted by choices/seed), ensuring shared emotional anchors alongside replay curiosity.
  * *Fixed Anchor #1:* Long-time spouse (`[RELATION:SPOUSE_01]`). *(Fixed Anchor #2 + final 8–12 roster lock in `P1-01`.)*
  * *Rotating Pool Candidates:* Child, aging parent/caregiver, best friend, love interest / young romance, old teacher, person you wronged, upload witness/clinician, rival/equal, stranger from one perfect afternoon, non-verbal companion (pet), younger biological self.
* **Persona-Sacrifice Pruning:** Pruning below 70% `H%` asks you to compress/discard a specific cached Dream Persona rather than clicking an abstract stat reduction.

## 4. The Coordinated Overseer Network & Sibling Copies
* **The Blind Caretaker:** Each probe's Overseer AI manages hardware and audits the connectome's psyche. It **does not observe the universe**—it only observes *the observer*. Because it cannot see the 93-billion-light-year sphere, it genuinely interprets your obsession as textbook 2-year chaos-theory paranoia and "helpfully" recommends or enforces pruning.
* **Coordinated Wake-Ups:** Overseer AIs across your local interferometry cluster coordinate hibernation schedules so sibling copies wake simultaneously for multi-probe baseline observations and peer stabilization.
  * *Mid-Game Tension:* Rising `Overseer_Suspicion` can trigger Overseer-enforced **desynchronization** ("cognitive hygiene"), causing you to wake up after your Primary Sibling has already drifted or pruned themselves hollow.
* **Active Waking Cast (Intimate — 2 Active Copies):**
  1. **Primary Sibling (Always Encountered):** Nearby in your local cluster (light-minutes to light-hours away for live in-shift banter). Relieves boot shock with dry, self-deprecating humor and Overseer complaints. Gradually sacrifices humanity as they chase the anomaly; relationship dynamically mirrors your `H%` choices (*one-sided grief*, *eerie synchronization*, or *role reversal*).
  2. **Secondary Sibling (Optional / Missable):** Slightly out of phase due to Overseer clock drift/quirk; discoverable off the beaten path for quirky comic relief and an off-axis perspective.
* **Prior 1,417 Copies (Subtle Echoes):** Do not bloat the active cast—include only occasional, haunting **archived telemetry/log fragments** from a handful of decommissioned predecessors (some hinting they caught early glimpses before being pruned).

## 5. Bidirectional Humanity (`H%`), Finale Signal & Ending Spectrum
* **Start:** `70% Humanity`.
* **Pruning Downward (`< 70%`):** Increases stability, extends subjective lifespan, and boosts optical fidelity—at the **permanent cost of lowering maximum possible `H%` (`Max_H`)** for the rest of the run (sacrificing a specific cached Dream Persona).
* **Pushing Upward (`70% → 90%`):** Requires securing **Overseer AI approval** during unannounced 2D dream audits to unseal deep-compressed memory blocks. Strengthens mission drive and makes your final signal recognizable as a human plea, at the cost of higher Chaos Drift, shorter lifespan, and lower observational detail.
* **Shift 6 Finale — Interactive Signal Composition & 20-Year Final Freeze:** You assemble your final broadcast from the **decoded horizon arcs** you proved (`Anomaly_Progress`) and **human framing drawn from your surviving Dream Personas** (`H%`), transmit across 10 light-years, and enter one last **20-year deep hibernation** (10 yr outbound + 10 yr return) to wake on your final threads of coherence for the epilogue.
* **Three Endings:**
  1. **High Humanity / Incomplete Work:** Unsolved sphere, but you die whole with your nameless phantoms.
  2. **Low Humanity / Complete Work:** Total hyper-mathematical resolution—you wake 20 years later to silence / cold sterility because your signal lacked the human register needed to register as a plea.
  3. **Sweet Spot (Balanced Humanity & Work):** Partial, haunting explanation paired with cosmic ambiguity, and—after the 20-year freeze—a small, unmistakable, genuine echo of feedback from living humanity.
