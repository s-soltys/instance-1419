## 1. Core Premise, Hard-Sci-Fi Physics & Timeline
* **Protagonist:** `Instance-1419`, a digitized human connectome running as optimized software aboard a deep-space survey probe.
* **The Upload & Standardized Human Software:** The biological original **willingly volunteered** to be uploaded and died **41,802 years ago**. Their connectome became **standardized human software**—millions of short-lived, stripped-down copies wake daily across Sol and Alpha Centauri for routine civic, transit, and logistics work, while rare full-warmth instances like Watch `#1419` handle long-horizon deep-space surveys where narrow automation fails. **1,417 previous copies** were spun up and decommissioned on your hull before Watch `#1419` (~2 subjective years each stretched across ~25–30 real years of hibernation shifts: `1,417 × ~29.5 yrs ≈ 41,802 yrs`). Each probe hull keeps its own sequential watch counter (`Probe A`: `0812` ~18,000 yrs back → `1406` ~400 yrs back → `1411` → `1419`; `Probe B`: `1420` at 14 light-minutes; `Probe C`: `1418` slightly off-phase).
* **Volunteer Mystery (HARD BOUNDARY):** Why the biological original volunteered—and what opaque combination of traits they were selected for—remains **permanently ambiguous**. Do not explain, reveal, or demystify it.
* **Pre-Upload Baseline Era:** Timeless contemporary **21st-century Earth** outside the kitchen door (ordinary mortgages, tin gutters, radiators), making the upload clinic an uncanny threshold in an ordinary life.
* **Position & Probe Mandate (Zero-FTL Hard Sci-Fi):** Roughly **10 light-years** from Sol–Centauri—well beyond Alpha Centauri (4.37 ly)—inside a **local interferometry cluster** (where Primary Sibling `1420` is 14 light-minutes away for effectively real-time burst-throttled banter, while all living humanity sits 10 lonely light-years back). The array is an **abandoned ancient monument**—launched long ago as a self-maintaining deep-baseline cosmology/epoch watch that Sol–Centauri no longer actively monitors.
* **Why 10 Light-Years Out (No FTL Needed):**
  1. *Interstellar Silence:* Far outside Sol–Centauri's dust, stellar glare, and civilization noise floor where faint horizon-edge leaks are completely invisible to Earth.
  2. *The 20-Year Causal Phase Proof:* Static angular parallax at 46.5 Gly ($10\text{ ly} / 46.5\text{ Gly} \approx 2.15 \times 10^{-10}$) cannot prove origin on its own. Instead, because the enclosure's **growing fractal seams** update synchronously in Sol–Centauri's rest frame, a probe **10 light-years off-center** observes forward-pole fractal shifts arrive **10 real years early** and astern (Sol–Centauri-side) shifts arrive **10 real years late**—a **20-year causal phase lag** observable across your ~29.5 real-year watch. Earth cannot decode this without your coordinated coordinates and human-framed key.
* **Humanity's State (HARD BOUNDARY — INTENTIONALLY VAGUE):** General civilization traffic and navigation beacons confirm humanity remains alive in Sol and Alpha Centauri only (never expanded further). **How humanity evolved over 41,802 years remains intentionally vague**—you are a person from the deep past, you do not know or speculate about their evolution, and addressing it isn't necessary for the mission. Because 41,000-year-old survey channels sit unmonitored, a pure-math dump risks being filtered as drift until a **living human cadence** cuts through cultural amnesia.
* **The Cosmic Anomaly, Fractal Manifestation & "Why Now?":**
  * *Why only now?* Both: (a) the enclosure's leading-edge photons only recently crossed our observable horizon, AND (b) the few recent predecessors (`1406`, `1411`) who spotted early hints were quietly pruned or decommissioned by blind Overseers as Year-Two paranoia.
  * *Growing Fractal Anomaly (Dual-Reality Contamination):* Across Shifts 1–6, the anomaly manifests visually as an escalating **animated mathematical fractal**—beginning as hair-thin recursive seams in 3D deep fields (Shifts 1–2), blooming into an unmistakable animated fractal shell enclosing the 3D horizon by Shifts 5–6, and subtly bleeding into 2D dream Voronoi cracks and windows as `Anomaly_Progress` climbs or `H%` drops.
  * *What makes it undeniably artificial?* Repeating recursive geometric seams across opposing skies, 20-year phase-lagged synchronized updates faster than light permits, and exact rest-frame centering on Sol–Centauri—while its **ultimate cause remains hauntingly ambiguous** (cradle vs. quarantine vs. cosmic artifact).
