# Game Concept Blueprint: Core Decisions

## 1. High-Level Premise
You are **Instance-1419**, a digitized human connectome running as optimized software aboard a deep-space survey probe. Your original biological self **willingly volunteered** to be uploaded—selected for an opaque, never-fully-explained combination of traits beyond raw intelligence—and died **41,802 years ago**. **1,417 previous copies** of you have already been spun up and decommissioned.

Positioned at the forefront of humanity's sensor network—roughly **2–3 light-years out**—your probe catches incoming photons from the edge of the observable universe **2–3 years before Earth can observe them**. While conducting meditative astronomical observations, you uncover an impossible cosmological anomaly: geometric patterns leaking across the particle horizon, forming a **nearly uniform spherical enclosure (~93 billion light-years across) centered precisely on Earth**.

---

## 2. Scope, Platform & Tech Stack
* **Target Scope:** Self-contained **2–4 hour indie experience** (premium or free passion project, zero ads).
* **Structure:** **Single Narrative Arc** — One continuous life as `Instance-1419`. No roguelike reset loop; every memory sacrificed and every time-skip carries permanent weight.
* **Platform & Orientation:** **Mobile (Landscape)** — Full-screen cinematic space vistas in waking mode, paired with full-screen painterly dreamscapes during sleep.
* **Recommended Engine:** **Godot 4 (Mobile Renderer)**.
* **Art Pipeline (Deferred):** Exact 2D/3D asset production scope is intentionally left flexible until a playable vertical slice proves the core loop.

---

## 3. World State & Opening Sequence
* **The Biological Anchor:** Opens with a warm, hand-painted 2D sequence of a brief, intimate conversation with close family members before your willing neural transfer.
* **The Hard Cut:** A clinical transition into a boot log revealing **41,802 years** have passed and **1,417 copies** preceded you. You wake up in shock, thrust into a cold 3D void to learn your job.
* **Permanently Lost Names:** Your personal memories are compressed into sanitized relational tags. While you can regain warmth, cadence, and emotional clarity, **you can never fully remember your loved ones' names**.
* **Local Humanity:** Humanity is **confirmed alive** and has evolved significantly over 41 millennia, but **never expanded beyond the Solar System and Alpha Centauri**—making the 93-billion-light-year sphere centered on their tiny two-star neighborhood feel unimaginably vast.

---

## 4. Dual-Reality Split & The 2-Year Chaos Ceiling
A simulated human mind is a chaotic dynamical system that typically breaks down after **~2 subjective years** due to chaos theory and psychic drift. You need a human psyche for the drive and intuition to do the work, but that psyche requires periodic rest to stay coherent.

### 3D Mode: Waking Exploration (Photorealistic)
* **Visuals:** Full-screen landscape cosmos grounded in real astronomy (JWST/Hubble deep-field nebulae, Gaia starfields, Einstein Rings, horizon leaks).
* **Gameplay (Open-Ended Suite):** Meditative, tactile astronomical observation exercises (exact suite of exercises to be prototyped—e.g., gravitational lensing alignment, spectral tuning, arc unwarping).
* **Shorter Time-Skips:** Between waking shifts, you hibernate for **months or years** (rather than millennia)—long enough for orbital alignments and horizon patches to shift while keeping you and your sibling copies on the same continuous timeline.

### 2D Mode: Dream Sequences (Painterly)
* **Neural Defragmentation:** Entering 2D dreams stabilizes **Chaos Drift** so your ~2-year clock doesn't collapse early.
* **The Dreamspace:** Inspired by *Disco Elysium*. You converse with your internal psyche and **nameless phantoms of your loved ones**.
* **Humanity-Driven Visual Degradation:** The 2D visuals of your loved ones dynamically evolve based on your Humanity (`H%`)—warm impasto brushwork at high `H%`, growing colder, thinner, and more scattered into abstract shards as `H%` drops.

---

## 5. The Overseer AI (Blind Caretaker)
* **Role:** Controls only mechanical probe systems and evaluates your psyche. **It is not its job to observe the universe**—it only observes *the observer* and maintains the hardware.
* **Dream Audits:** You rarely interact with it during waking shifts. Instead, it intervenes unannounced **inside your 2D dreams** to run psychological stability tests.
* **Tragic Banality:** Because the Overseer cannot see the cosmic sphere, it genuinely interprets your growing obsession with a 93-billion-light-year shell around Earth as textbook 2-year chaos-theory paranoia—and attempts to "help" by recommending or enforcing corrective pruning.

---

## 6. Sibling Copies: Comic Relief & Estrangement
During 3D waking shifts, you can connect with a tight cast of sibling copies doing the same lonely job:

* **Primary Sibling (Always Encountered):** Arrives early to relieve your initial boot shock with dry, self-deprecating humor and shared complaints about the Overseer. Over time, as they push to resolve the horizon anomaly, they gradually sacrifice their own humanity and lose their humor. Your evolving relationship mirrors your own `H%` choices (one-sided grief if you stay human, eerie synchronization if you both go hollow, or role reversal if you prune first).
* **Secondary Sibling (Optional / Missable):** A hidden, eccentric copy you may discover off the beaten path—offering extra comic relief and a quirky side perspective on the Overseer.

---

## 7. Core Dilemma: Bidirectional Humanity (`H%`)
You start the game at **70% Humanity**, with the ability to move in either direction:

* **Pruning Downward (< 70%) — Permanent Ceiling Drop:**
  * *Benefits:* Increases connectome stability, extends subjective lifespan, and frees up compute for **razor-sharp observational fidelity** of the cosmic sphere.
  * *Permanent Cost:* Every time you prune below 70%, you **permanently lower your maximum possible `H%`**. Even if you recover memories later, you carry the scar for the rest of the run.
* **Pushing Upward (70% → 90%) — Overseer Approval:**
  * *Mechanism:* Difficult to achieve; requires petitioning and securing **Overseer AI approval** during 2D dream audits to unseal deep-compressed memory blocks.
  * *Benefits:* Strengthens your **drive and connection to the mission**, and makes your final signal **more understandable to evolved Sol–Centauri humanity**.
  * *Cost:* Increases psychic instability (Chaos Drift), decreases remaining subjective lifespan, and reduces observational detail of the anomaly.

---

## 8. Ending Spectrum
The conclusion depends on the balance between **observational work completed** and **remaining Humanity (`H%`)**:

1. **High Humanity / Incomplete Work:** You never fully solve the sphere, but you die whole—reconnected with your humanity and nameless phantoms.
2. **Low Humanity / Complete Work:** Total observational resolution, delivered as a cold, hyper-mathematical, non-emotional truth—and too sterile for Earth to recognize as a human plea.
3. **The Sweet Spot (Balanced Humanity & Work):** A partial, haunting explanation balanced with cosmic ambiguity, paired with a small, genuine echo of feedback from living humanity.
